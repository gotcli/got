# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in GOT Community a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and welcoming.
- Give and accept technical feedback in good faith.
- Focus discussion on the project and its community.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's private information.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, personal attacks, and other
unprofessional conduct are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and may restrict
participation for conduct they consider inappropriate, threatening, offensive,
or harmful.

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainers through a private
contact method listed on the maintainers' GitHub profiles. Do not include
sensitive personal information in a public issue. Maintainers will respect the
privacy and safety of reporters as far as reasonably possible.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in project spaces and when an individual publicly
represents GOT Community.
